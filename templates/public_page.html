ABOUT LEARN4ALL

Education should reveal possibility, not limits.

Learn4All is a Python-based inclusive learning progress and resource planner designed to turn real assessment data into clear next actions.

THE IDEA

From marks to meaning.

Students enter their own assessment results. The system calculates performance by subject and topic, identifies gaps and surfaces matching learning resources.

THE APPROACH

Personalized, not pre-filled.

A new student starts with an intentionally empty learning dashboard. The interface changes as their real data changes, so the story belongs to the learner.

INCLUSIVE DESIGN

One platform, different learning needs.

Accessibility preferences can be saved to the learner profile. When recommendations are generated, accessible resources are prioritized when available.
